## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏘 [AavaasIQ](https://github.com/gangotrigupta-61/AavaasIQ) — Smart Residential Society Management Platform

Managing a housing society today means scattered paper registers, WhatsApp disputes, and no central system. AavaasIQ brings residents, management, security, and service partners onto one unified digital platform.

**Problem it solves:** Visitor & parcel logging, maintenance dues tracking, complaint management, society notices, and service partner bookings — all in one place.

**Tech:** `Next.js` · `React 19` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS v4` · `Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth)`

🔗 [Live Demo](https://aavaas-iq.vercel.app/) · [Repository](https://github.com/gangotrigupta-61/AavaasIQ)

---

### 🔎 [FoundifyX](https://github.com/gangotrigupta-61/foundifyX) — Campus Lost & Found Platform

Lost items on a campus rarely get returned because there is no central system. FoundifyX lets users report lost or found items, automatically checks new reports against existing records, and coordinates secure item recovery.

**Problem it solves:** Eliminates chaos around campus lost & found through real-time Firestore matching and private Gmail-based coordination between finders and owners.

**Tech:** `HTML` · `CSS` · `JavaScript` · `Firebase` · `Firestore`

🔗 [Live Demo](https://campus-lost-found-476c7.web.app) · [Demo Video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1DFR3w1Ql7bMuuNsjP1ixv8VdlMLOeq8u/view?usp=sharing) · [Repository](https://github.com/gangotrigupta-61/foundifyX)

---

### 📋 [NOVA](https://github.com/gangotrigupta-61/NOVA-) — Team Project Management & Kanban Board

Teams waste time on messy task coordination and unclear ownership. NOVA is a full-stack MERN application that gives teams a structured Kanban workflow with real-time task tracking.

**Problem it solves:** Project creation, Kanban board (To Do / In Progress / Done), task assignment with priorities and due dates, and team member management — all secured with JWT authentication.

**Tech:** `React.js` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MongoDB` · `JWT` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Vercel + Render`

🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/gangotrigupta-61/NOVA-)

---

### 🌳 [MyWoods](https://github.com/gangotrigupta-61/Mywoods-Website) — Full Stack MERN Wood Collection Platform

An e-commerce platform for browsing and managing premium wood collections, featuring secure authentication, an Admin CMS, image uploads, and a RESTful API backend.

**Problem it solves:** Provides a complete buyer-facing storefront with product listings and an admin panel for managing inventory — built end-to-end with the MERN stack.

**Tech:** `React.js` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MongoDB` · `JWT` · `Cloudinary`

🔗 [Live Demo](https://timbercraftwoods.vercel.app) · [Frontend Repo](https://github.com/gangotrigupta-61/Mywoods-Website) · [Backend Repo](https://github.com/gangotrigupta-61/MyWoods-Backend)
